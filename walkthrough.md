# Tenant Turner Demo walkthrough

The recording follows a property manager through a rental’s setup, what happens when leads respond, and how the team tracks showings and access. Times are measured from the beginning of the video. Clicking a screenshot opens the full-size image. [Watch the original recording](https://vimeo.com/688083346?fl=pl&fe=cm)

## 00:00–01:24 · Open the Rentals dashboard

The Rentals page shows property cards with a photo, status and links to **Leads** and **Settings**. The top controls filter by assignee, include inactive rentals and search by property. Wait List, alert and hardware badges give the team a quick view of each rental.

Larry describes the path a prospective renter takes. Tenant Turner supplies contact channels for listings, responds to inquiries, asks qualification questions and directs qualifying leads to available showing times. The presenter also describes confirmation reminders and follow-up messages with an application link and a request for feedback.

![Rentals dashboard with property cards and status badges](screenshots/0000-rentals.jpg)

## 01:24–02:23 · Set the assignee, listing details and syndication

From the selected card, open **Settings**. The rental header has **Leads**, **Owner Report**, **Availability** and **Settings** tabs. The first settings panel shows whether the rental is active and which team member is assigned. The presenter opens the assignee selector and explains how the assigned person’s calendar affects in-person showings.

![Rental status, assignment and marketing settings](screenshots/0125-rental-settings.jpg)

Under **Marketing**, review lease term, rent, deposit, move-in date, bed and bath count, square footage, listing title and description. Several fields are greyed out in the recording because the listing comes from property management software. The presenter says rentals can also be entered manually. The next settings show photos, virtual tour links, amenities, utilities and address information.

![Marketing fields for the selected rental](screenshots/0142-marketing.jpg)

![Rental photo gallery](screenshots/0200-photos.jpg)

The **Syndication** section has a switch for each listing site. The presenter toggles destinations on the sample rental to illustrate that distribution can differ by listing.

![Listing site switches](screenshots/0215-syndication.jpg)

## 02:23–03:04 · Set prequalification questions

The **Qualifying** section contains configurable questions and restrictions. The recording scrolls through move-in timing, income relative to rent, co-signers, pet count and types, and other questions. The presenter describes account defaults and per-rental settings.

![Qualifying restrictions in rental settings](screenshots/0232-qualifying.jpg)

**Custom Questions** shows how an answer can be marked as disqualifying. The questions and values in the screenshots are examples from this recording.

![Custom questions and an answer marked as disqualifying](screenshots/0253-custom-questions.jpg)

## 03:04–03:28 · Offer a wait list before showings begin

Under **Viewing Methods**, the presenter switches on **Wait List**, enters a short message and then switches it off. The form explains that current leads can be notified when viewings become available. The rental can take inquiries while it is still being prepared.

![Wait List message and viewing method settings](screenshots/0314-wait-list.jpg)

## 03:28–06:00 · Configure self-access showings

**Self-Access Viewings** shows a viewing method, an appointment setting, an assigned device, instructions and a government ID requirement. A notice on the screen discusses rental scams. The viewing type menu shows electronic options and alternatives such as instructions only and a combination lockbox.

![Self-access settings and ID requirement](screenshots/0337-self-access.jpg)

![Viewing type selector](screenshots/0352-viewing-types.jpg)

While this screen stays open, the presenter explains that the platform can review lead data for signs of fraud before scheduling and alert staff when a lead needs attention. The presenter describes limited-time access at the appointment and then opens the device selector, which groups available devices and devices already assigned to rentals.

![Device assignment selector](screenshots/0548-device-assignment.jpg)

## 06:00–06:58 · Configure in-person showings and availability

**In-Person Showings** has controls for maximum showings per time, duration, scheduling notice, a buffer before the trip, and optional notices to occupants. The presenter opens the limit menu to show how the team can limit showings or trips to a rental.

![In-person showing controls](screenshots/0605-in-person.jpg)

![Showing and trip limit options](screenshots/0620-showing-limit.jpg)

**General Availability** lists team members and opens their availability editors. The presenter describes checking connected Google or Outlook calendars. **Rental-Specific Availability** can add a repeating time block and assign it to a property manager. The visible form has fields for day, start and end times, appointment handling and maximum showings per slot.

![General availability and team assignments](screenshots/0633-general-availability.jpg)

![Rental-specific availability](screenshots/0648-rental-availability.jpg)

At the bottom of Settings, **Application & Fees** shows an application link and fee fields. **Save Rental Settings** is the final action on this page. The recording then moves to the rental’s Leads tab.

![Application link, fee fields and Save Rental Settings](screenshots/0655-application.jpg)

## 06:58–07:42 · Review the lead queue and an individual lead

The **Leads** tab lists qualification, stage, recent activity and row actions. Controls above the table sort leads, include archived entries and search by name. Green markers identify leads that meet the selected criteria and get access to scheduling. The presenter describes how an ineligible lead is archived and shown the restriction that applies.

![Lead queue with qualification, stage and activity columns](screenshots/0712-lead-queue.jpg)

The presenter opens a lead from the search. The profile moves from contact actions, notes and viewing feedback to an ID review and viewing history, then to **Pre-Qualification** cards. Those cards group lease preference, financial and legal history, source of rent and source of lead. The archive masks the identifying details and access values displayed on the profile.

## 07:42–08:35 · Share rental progress with the owner

The **Owner Report** tab opens a report for the same rental. A switch schedules automatic delivery by email. The report can include feedback. Its metrics compare the last seven days and totals for leads, prequalified leads, scheduled leads and viewings. The presenter opens a linked feedback entry to show how staff can hide it from the owner report.

![Owner report delivery controls](screenshots/0745-owner-schedule.jpg)

![Owner report metrics and feedback](screenshots/0818-owner-metrics.jpg)

## 08:35–09:33 · Browse reports, schedule delivery and inspect lead sources

The main **Reports** page separates the **Standard** catalog from **Scheduled** reports. The catalog groups reports by rental, viewing, lockbox and user/account activity. The scheduling screen includes the weekly owner report day and time, plus daily, weekly or monthly delivery options for standard reports.

![Standard report catalog](screenshots/0836-report-catalog.jpg)

![Scheduled owner and standard reports](screenshots/0842-scheduled-reports.jpg)

Open **Rental Activity Report** to see totals per rental and controls for rental, user, date range and active status. The result table has Copy, CSV, Excel, PDF and Print controls. Then open **Lead Source Report** and change the start date before filtering again. The expanded table separates status, channel, source and partner. The archive masks lead names and contact details in this report.

![Filtered Rental Activity Report](screenshots/0853-rental-activity.jpg)

![Lead Source Report with private columns masked](screenshots/0926-lead-source.jpg)

## 09:33–10:21 · Review access devices and authorized contacts

The **Access** area contains tabs for CodeBox, SentriLock, PointCentral and **Authorized Contacts**. The device cards group assigned and unassigned inventory. The presenter opens **Send CodeBox Code** to show the form for a date, code type and recipient information. The recording shows the form and returns to the device list.

![Access inventory with device credentials masked](screenshots/0938-access.jpg)

![Send CodeBox Code form](screenshots/0950-send-code.jpg)

In **Authorized Contacts**, the presenter switches to the contact list and opens **Edit Authorized Contact**. The presenter contrasts one-time access for a vendor with recurring access for an authorized contact, then points out the option to remove a contact. The archive masks the contact records.

![Authorized Contacts tab](screenshots/1005-authorized-contacts.jpg)

![Edit Authorized Contact screen](screenshots/1018-edit-contact.jpg)

## 10:21–11:25.500 · Return to Rentals and open support

The presenter returns to the Rentals dashboard for the wrap-up. The support chat launcher opens a panel on the right, then closes before the recording ends back on the property grid.
